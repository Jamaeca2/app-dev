# My Favorite Movie: Saw

**Saw** is a 2004 psychological horror film directed by *James Wan* and written by Leigh Whannell. It follows two strangers who wake up chained in a dilapidated bathroom, forced to play a deadly game orchestrated by the notorious Jigsaw Killer.

## Why I Love It
* **Mind-Bending Plot:** A tightly wound thriller with twists and turns that keep you guessing until the very end.
* **Psychological Tension:** Focuses heavily on moral dilemmas, survival, and human instinct rather than just traditional scares.
* **Iconic Atmosphere:** Gritty cinematography, a suspenseful soundtrack, and brilliant puzzle-solving elements.

## Main Characters
1. John Kramer (Jigsaw)
2. Dr. Lawrence Gordon
3. Adam Stanheight
4. Detective David Tapp
5. Amanda Young

> "I want to play a game." — Jigsaw
